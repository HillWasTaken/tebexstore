Questions

How do I get a script after buying it?

At checkout you sign in with your Cfx.re account. After payment the script appears in your keymaster under Granted Assets, ready to download and add to your server.

Which frameworks are supported?

Each product page lists what it works with. Most scripts support ESX, QBCore and Qbox out of the box, and use ox_lib where that makes sense.

Can I change the code?

Logic is protected by escrow, but everything you’d normally want to change is open: config files, language files and client-side UI.

How are Discord bots delivered?

You receive the bot files and a setup guide by email after payment. Prefer not to host it yourself? Ask on Discord about hosted options.

What if something doesn’t work?

Open a ticket on Discord with your server log. Because these are digital products, purchases are final once delivered, but every bug that’s on our side gets fixed.
